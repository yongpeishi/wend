One-line: a row of the day plan — use it for anything with a time against it, on paper or on the deep-leaf outdoor surface.

```jsx
<TimeRow time="10:15–11:40" title="Nanzen-ji" meta="east · 12 min walk from the station" state="decided" />
```

Stop states reuse the trail palette: decided (leaf), open (apricot ring — where you are now), waiting (pale), destination (plum). Pass `onDark` inside `<Card tone="inverse">`.
