Wend

Design system

v1.0 · from option 7a · michikusa

Wend carries a daydream through to something you can hold up on a street corner and follow. Planning is done at a desk, comparing and regrouping ideas; the finished plan is read on a phone while walking. The brand's one figure is the dotted trail. Everything else stays quiet, legible, and out of the way. One typeface, one accent, generous space.

01 Principles

Nothing is discarded

Narrowing hides options, never deletes them. Every view has a way back to the wider one, and anything set aside can be picked up again.

Grouping is the mechanic

Ideas are gathered, split, regrouped and compared constantly. Anything that can be grouped can be ungrouped just as cheaply, any group can be duplicated or forked, and two versions can sit side by side until someone decides.

Legible before lovely

Times, platforms and addresses are read in bright sun, in a hurry. They set the type floor.

02 The mark

Primary · on paper

Reversed · on deep leaf

One colour · stamps, favicon

min 24px

Small size · thicker stroke

Anatomy

Stops on a dotted trail, in order: solid green — decided; apricot ring — where you are, still open; plum — the destination, waiting. The dashes are footsteps: 1px dot on a 7–8px gap, round caps.

Clear space on all sides equals the diameter of the largest dot. Never straighten the path, never fill the apricot ring. The mark itself is always drawn with three stops; in-product trails may carry as many as the flow needs.

Wordmark

Wend

Atkinson Hyperlegible Bold, all caps, 0.26em tracking. The tracking is fixed at every size — it is the wordmark's signature, and it makes the letters read as separate steps. Lock-up: mark left, wordmark right, gap equal to the cap height.

Apricot is the only warm colour and it means one thing: this is where you are deciding. It is a shape, never a text colour — a ring, a 3px focus outline, an underline. It clears AA against neither paper nor deep leaf, so nothing that must be read is set in it. Ratios above are measured against paper #F0F3EE; all pass AA for normal text.

04 Type

Atkinson Hyperlegible, Regular and Bold, everywhere. Its slashed zero, tailed l and serifed I are why it was chosen — an itinerary is mostly numbers. DM Mono appears only for codes, coordinates and labels.

07 Motion & voice

Motion

One gesture: the trail draws itself forward when a step completes, dot by dot, 420ms, ease-out. Going back plays it in reverse at the same speed — never faster, so returning feels as considered as advancing.

Everything else is a 160ms opacity change. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: the trail redraws instantly, dots still change state.

Voice

Playful, forgiving, fond of detours. Short sentences, plain words, second person. Never urgent, never scarce — no countdowns, no "only 2 left".

Yes: "You'll get there. Slowly is fine."

Yes: "Kept — it's waiting in your shortlist."

No: "Optimise your itinerary now."
